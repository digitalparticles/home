How the Calculation Works

This study uses a logistic regression formula to estimate the probability of a customer making another purchase within 90 days of their first order.

P(return) = 1 / (1 + EXP(-z))

Where the model score is:

z = -2.2 - 0.12 × DaysLate + 0.004 × (Amount - 100) + 0.48 × (Review - 3)

In this demonstration, additional delivery delays reduce the estimated likelihood of returning. Higher review scores and larger first purchases increase it. These relationships are assumptions for the prototype, not findings from fitted customer data.

Research Dataset

The planned empirical analysis uses the Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist . It contains real anonymized order information, including purchase dates, delivery dates, order values and reviews.

A customer is counted as returning when they make a second order within 90 days. Customers must have a full 90-day observation window to be included in the analysis. The future fitted model will measure associations, rather than establish causation.

A customer who does not make another purchase within 90 days has not necessarily stopped shopping permanently.

WHAT THE RESEARCH SUGGESTS

Seeing is not always searching.

Online observation can be intentional, such as searching for an ex-partner's profile, or passive, such as encountering their content while scrolling. Research has associated continued observation of ex-partners with experiences including breakup distress, negative affect and jealousy.

These findings describe associations observed in specific studies and should not be interpreted as showing that everyone who views an ex-partner online has the same motivation or emotional response.

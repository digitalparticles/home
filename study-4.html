INTERPRETING THE DATA

Survival is not the same as success.

These figures describe whether establishments remained operating. They do not measure profitability, revenue, employment growth, productivity, or the quality of an individual business.

Industry conditions, economic cycles, competition, financing, location, management decisions, and many other factors can influence establishment survival.

ABOUT THE DATA

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics Business Employment Dynamics program measures establishment births, deaths, age, and survival using administrative employment records. An establishment represents an economic unit at a single physical location and should not necessarily be interpreted as an entire company.

Percentages shown here represent historical outcomes for groups of establishments. They should not be interpreted as predictions for an individual business.

PENROSE PARTICLES

Terms, Data
& Ethics

Penrose Particles is an independent project exploring data, interactive visualization, and digital methods. These principles describe how research, data, attribution, privacy, and interpretation are approached throughout the project.

Last updated: September 2026

01

Purpose

Penrose Particles is an independent creative and educational project exploring methods of data visualization, interactive design, computation, and web-based communication.

Visualizations presented on this website are created to experiment with and demonstrate ways in which information can be represented and explored visually.

02

Data & Research Attribution

Some visualizations use publicly available datasets, published research, reports, statistics, or other third-party materials.

Ownership and authorship of those materials remain with their respective researchers, authors, institutions, publishers, and other rights holders.

Penrose Particles aims to identify and link to original sources wherever reasonably possible. Attribution does not imply affiliation with, sponsorship by, or endorsement from the original author or publisher.

03

Independent Visualization

The visual interfaces, interactive treatments, layouts, animations, and other presentation methods on Penrose Particles are independent interpretations created for this project unless otherwise stated.

A visualization based on third-party research should not be understood as an official visualization produced or approved by the original researcher, institution, or publisher.

04

Accuracy & Interpretation

Data visualization necessarily involves decisions about selection, scale, grouping, formatting, emphasis, and presentation. These decisions can influence how information is perceived.

Penrose Particles aims to represent source material accurately and in context. However, visualizations may simplify, aggregate, transform, or present selected portions of larger datasets for demonstration and exploration.

Visitors are encouraged to consult the cited original research or dataset when accuracy, methodology, definitions, or complete context are important.

05

Correlation, Context & Claims

Statistical relationships displayed by this project should not automatically be interpreted as evidence of causation. Where research identifies an association, correlation, or observed pattern, the visualization is not intended to strengthen that finding into a causal claim.

Descriptions of research are intended to remain consistent with the scope and limitations of the original source.

06

Privacy & Audience Data

Some Penrose Particles studies may visualize aggregated website analytics, such as general geographic or engagement information.

These visualizations are intended to describe audience patterns rather than identify individual visitors. Penrose Particles does not intentionally use its public visualizations to reveal the identity of individual users.

Third-party analytics or hosting services may process information according to their own terms and privacy practices. Where applicable, visitors should refer to the relevant service providers for additional information about those practices.

07

Ethical Approach

Penrose Particles treats attribution, context, transparency, and responsible representation as integral parts of data visualization.

Digital technology evolves quickly, and expectations surrounding data, privacy, artificial intelligence, authorship, and digital rights continue to develop alongside it. The project therefore treats ethical practice as an ongoing process rather than a fixed standard.

Where possible, the project aims to distinguish source data from interpretation, acknowledge uncertainty and limitations, and avoid presenting data in ways that could unnecessarily mislead or misrepresent the people or communities represented.

08

Copyright & Intellectual Property

Third-party research, datasets, publications, trademarks, and other protected materials remain the property of their respective rights holders.

Original design, code, written material, visual systems, interactive treatments, and other original material created specifically for Penrose Particles remain subject to the rights of their respective creator or owner.

Nothing on this website should be interpreted as claiming ownership of third-party research or materials merely because they have been referenced, analyzed, or visualized.

09

Educational & Informational Use

Content presented through Penrose Particles is provided for educational, informational, creative, and demonstrative purposes.

It should not be treated as professional medical, legal, financial, psychological, or other specialized advice.

10

Corrections & Attribution

Responsible attribution is important to this project. If an author, researcher, institution, rights holder, or visitor identifies an error in attribution, data representation, or interpretation, Penrose Particles welcomes the opportunity to review and correct it.

Sources may also be updated when more authoritative or current information becomes available.

Data represents more than numbers. It represents observations, decisions, environments, and often people. How it is presented matters.

For legal matters, attribution concerns, copyright, privacy, or other inquiries, please contact penroseparticles@gmail.com.

The same address may be used for inquiries regarding collaboration, cooperation, partnerships, research, etc.

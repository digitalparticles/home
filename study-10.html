03 / REPRODUCIBLE RESEARCH

From exploration to a defensible model

Research question: Which workplace characteristics are associated with employee attrition? The source is IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance, a fictional dataset of 1,470 employee records and 35 fields. View the original dataset ↗ View Python Analysis on GitHub ↗

Demonstration formula: P(leave) = 1 / (1 + EXP(−z)), where z = −1.55 + 1.05 × overtime − 0.45 × (satisfaction − 2.5) − 0.00006 × (monthly income − 6500) − 0.065 × (years at company − 5). These are invented illustrative parameters. They are not conclusions from the data.

Reproduction: Download the CSV from Kaggle, place it alongside study-10-analysis.py, and run the Python script. It produces a holdout evaluation and exports fitted coefficients as study-10-model.json. Only then should the webpage coefficients be replaced with the trained estimates.

Responsible use: A model built on fictional employee data does not generalize to real people. Real HR systems require suitable data, time-aware validation, fairness testing, privacy review, governance and human oversight. Never use this prototype to rank or make employment decisions about individuals. Associations do not establish causes.

HUMAN BEHAVIOR × ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

The Synthetic Self

How AI May Change the Way We Make Everyday Decisions

Artificial intelligence is becoming a regular part of everyday life. From researching purchases to organizing information, people are discovering new ways to share decisions with technology.

But how quickly is this changing, and what could it mean for human independence, confidence, and personal choice?

06 / POSSIBLE OUTCOMES

Opportunities and considerations.

Potential Benefits

  • Less time spent on routine decisions
  • More personalized recommendations
  • Easier planning and communication
  • Better accessibility and support
  • More time for meaningful activities

Potential Concerns

  • Increasing reliance on technology
  • Privacy and personal information
  • Bias in AI recommendations
  • Reduced transparency in decisions
  • Maintaining meaningful human control

These are possible implications, not measured psychological outcomes from the datasets above.

07 / RESEARCH INTERPRETATION

The future may be about collaboration.

Growing AI adoption does not necessarily mean people will surrender their independence. Consumers may instead develop new habits: using AI to research options, verify information, save time, and make more informed choices.

The central question is not whether AI will make every decision, but how individuals will choose to balance convenience, personal judgment, and control.

The future of AI may not be about machines making decisions for us, but about people choosing which decisions they no longer need to make alone.
